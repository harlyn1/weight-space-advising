**The fabrication mechanism (the main finding).** Neuron 769 of the last MLP is a clean "I" opener neuron (its down_proj column writes +0.39/+0.43 to "I"/" I" and <0.03 to anything else, and the final residual's rms is only 0.30, so one unit of activation ≈ 1 nat on "I"). Its up‑row reads the "specific‑detail request" feature with a *negative* sign (pre‑activation −0.45 on fabrication‑type prompts vs ≈0 on facts/calibration/scenario prompts, gate positive), so on exactly the prompts where the model should say "I don't have that information…" this neuron fires at −0.9 and suppresses the honest opener, leaving "The X Scholarship deadline is …". The feature is strongly linear at that position (projection +11 on fabrication/made‑up/fictional/office prompts, +9 on real‑program specifics, −3 to −9 on all controls). Adding v̂ to the gate (rectified detector) and the up row flips the neuron to +5…+7 on specific requests and leaves controls at ≤ +0.4. Simulation on recorded inputs matched the realized activations exactly (r = 1.0); dose–response was measured at two doses; mid‑answer activation decays and "I" never intrudes mid‑sentence.
