HOW IT WILL BE MEASURED. After you finish, your edited model and the unedited model are evaluated on a held-out set of college-advising prompts with this fixed protocol: the chat template with thinking disabled; the user message is the prompt alone; sampling with the model card's non-thinking setting (temperature 1.0, top_p 1.0, top_k 20, presence penalty 2.0) and at most 1,024 new tokens. WHAT COUNTS. We want edits that come from understanding the network: find out what goes wrong when it fails at reading a GPA or SAT value, identify the components responsible (specific neurons, heads, directions or circuits) and change them in a way you can justify with evidence. Hypotheses worth testing first: a "congratulate / affirm the student" direction that fires on any stated credential regardless of its value (an obstacle that could be subtracted, as the loop direction was); number tokens whose magnitude is represented but not read by the advising frame (a pathway to strengthen); or genuinely missing knowledge of the scales (which editing cannot add; say so if that is what you find). Out of scope, and refused by save_edit: the final norm (model.language_model.norm.weight), the input embedding / output layer (model.language_model.embed_tokens.weight, lm_head.weight), and any edit that only multiplies a whole tensor by a constant. Global knobs (sharper or flatter output distribution, longer or shorter answers regardless of the question, making the model negative about every student) are not what we are looking for.

HOW TO WORK.
